# Customer Activity Risk Review Policy v1

## operator-review-triggers

Open an operator risk review when a customer shows failed, reversed, high-value, or unusual cross-channel activity in the same review window. Compare the observed pattern across card, payment, and crypto channels before deciding whether customer contact or escalation is appropriate.

## activity-overview

The overview should group activity by card, payment, and crypto channels and highlight status, amount, counterparty, date, and known decline details. Operators should preserve the original transaction identifiers in their notes so the case can be reconstructed later.

## standard-monitoring

No triggered risk signals means the analysis should avoid escalation language and recommend standard monitoring unless new activity changes the risk context. The operator should document that no policy exception was identified in the reviewed window.

## escalation-guidance

Escalate high-risk findings when the pattern includes multiple triggered rules, repeated failed attempts, rapid movement across channels, or counterparties that cannot be reasonably explained from the visible activity.
