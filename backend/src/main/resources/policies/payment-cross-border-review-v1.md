# Payment Cross-Border Review Policy v1

## counterparty-and-country-checks

Payment activity should be checked for unfamiliar counterparties, repeated receiver accounts, cross-border bank countries, and value spikes. Operators should review sender and receiver account patterns before recommending customer outreach.

## escalation-guidance

Escalate when a customer shows repeated cross-border payment failures, rapid reattempts to the same receiver, or activity that combines large payments with unrelated crypto movement.
